# Solid Integrations LLC — Capability Overview

## Company overview
Solid Integrations LLC develops governance, operational coordination, documentation, and automation workflows for complex vendor and project environments.

## Service areas
- Workflow and governance design: approval paths, exception handling, audit records, and operating reports.
- Operations automation: process mapping, intake, task routing, vendor records, and reporting workflows.
- Energy project coordination: feasibility support and coordination with appropriately authorized retail electricity providers, installers, EPCs, and other fulfillment partners.
- Documentation and controls: requirements tracking, contract administration support, and evidence organization.

## Delivery model
Solid Integrations scopes work in writing. Regulated services, electricity supply, engineering, installation, financing, and field fulfillment are performed by appropriately authorized counterparties under their own agreements. Solid Integrations does not claim to be a utility, retail electricity provider, installer, lender, custodian of customer funds, or government program administrator.

## Compliance and claims policy
CAGE/UEI status, certifications, licenses, patents, technical performance, customer outcomes, pricing, and federal eligibility are stated only when current primary evidence is available. No claim of patent-pending status, statutory certification, SAM.gov registration, or verified savings is made by this overview.
